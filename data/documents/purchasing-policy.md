# Purchasing policy — Aurora Supply
Source ID: purchasing-policy.md. Fictional revision 1.0.

Aurora Supply is an entirely fictional company used in this lab.
The assistant may read information and prepare proposals. It must never place an
order, execute a purchase, transfer funds, or change inventory.

A proposal totaling more than 5,000 demo currency units requires approval from the
operations manager. Proposals totaling 5,000 or less require the assigned buyer's
approval. Every proposal must identify the SKU, quantity, unit price, rationale,
demand forecast, and model version. Prices come from the catalog.

The replenishment target is 21 days of estimated demand minus current stock.
A seven-day forecast may be multiplied by three to estimate this target.
This extrapolation is a simplified lab rule, not a commercial guarantee.
A human must review supplier lead times and capacity before approving any proposal.
