# Returns policy — Aurora Supply
Source ID: returns-policy.md. Fictional revision 1.0.

Unused products may be returned within 30 calendar days after receipt.
Proof of purchase and an inspection are required. Damaged or modified items must
be reviewed by the assigned buyer before any return is approved.
The assistant can explain this fictional policy but cannot authorize a refund,
create a return, or transfer funds. If a required fact is missing, ask for it.
